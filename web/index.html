Enter a matrix on the left (or pick an example), then press Solve. You will get:

  • every row operation, in the lecture's notation — L₂ → L₂ − 2·L₁ — with the pivot and the changed rows highlighted;
  • the echelon form and the canonical echelon form (מטריצה מדורגת / מדורגת קנונית);
  • the solution: one, none, or infinitely many with parameters α, β, … — checked by substituting it back.

Practice mode hides the steps and reveals them one at a time: work out the next operation on paper first.

Only the three elementary row operations of lecture 3 (שיעור 3) are used; a pivot of ±1 below is swapped up, as by hand. The answer is substituted back into your original equations before it is shown.
